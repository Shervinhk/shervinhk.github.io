<p class="f4 lh-copy mid-gray">My interests split fairly clearly into two groups. Some are close to my work and shape the kinds of technical problems I want to spend time on. Others sit outside work, but matter just as much to how I think and pay attention.</p>

<div class="mv5">
  <h2 class="f2 mb3">Technical Interests</h2>
  <p class="f4 lh-copy mid-gray">A lot of my curiosity sits around technical questions in statistics, machine learning, and the sciences. I’m especially drawn to both parametric and non parametric statistics, computational biology, and problems in biology and medicine where modeling can help make complex systems more understandable.</p>
  <p class="f4 lh-copy mid-gray">Those are the areas where I most naturally want to read more, build things, and follow difficult open ended questions over time.</p>
</div>
